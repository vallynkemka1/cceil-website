Who We Are

A Community Built on Belonging

CCEIL was born from a simple idea â€” that nobody should navigate a new country alone. Founded by Cameroonian families who understood the challenges of immigration firsthand, we grew from an informal support group into a registered nonprofit serving all immigrants in Lethbridge.

We address real barriers: racism, language challenges, employment gaps, and cultural adjustment â€” through mentorship, advocacy, housing networks, and programming that uplifts the whole person.
